Chapter 2

Research verse

Four papers, one question running through them: when should we trust what a model tells us?

First author · Preparing submission to IEEE Access

When is calibration measurable? A Sample-Size–Controlled Study of Confidence Reliability in 3D Skeleton Action Recognition

N. Vytla, K. K. Eepuri

A measurement study of how reliable model confidence is across MSR Action3D, MSR DailyActivity3D and NTU RGB+D 60/120. It compares temperature scaling, vector scaling, label smoothing, focal loss, MC dropout and multi-stream ensembling using ECE, Brier score, NLL, reliability diagrams and bootstrap confidence intervals. A null-model simulation separates real miscalibration from ECE estimator bias at small test sizes.

Under peer review

3 manuscripts · co-authored

  1. Under peer reviewBMC Medical ImagingSecond author

    BTFuseViT: A Vision Transformer with Multi-Dataset Fusion and Phased Transfer Learning for Multi-Class Brain Tumor Classification

    K. K. Eepuri, N. Vytla, K. Rajendra Prasad, P. Thottempudi, K. Siripuri

    A ViT-B/16 at 384×384, fine-tuned on three fused brain MRI datasets with phased training (asymmetric focal loss, bf16 mixed precision, stochastic weight averaging) and temperature-scaled test-time augmentation. It separates glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor and no tumor, with Grad-CAM++ for explanations.

    • 99.17% accuracy
    • 0.9995 macro AUC
    • 98.67% on BRISC 2025 holdout
    • 86.6M params vs ~450M for a 10-model ensemble (97.83%)
  2. Under peer reviewScientific ReportsCo-author

    HGRL-ATN: A Hierarchical Graph Reinforcement Learning Framework for Trustworthy UAV Navigation under GPS Spoofing Attacks

    K. K. Eepuri, R. Konari, S. Prasad J, N. Vytla, H. A. Sk, M. SirElkhatim, W. Eltayeb

    Hierarchical graph reinforcement learning with trust estimation, so a UAV can keep navigating when the GPS signal it receives is being spoofed.

    • 0.965 spoofing-detection F1
    • Hierarchical graph RL + trust estimation
  3. Under peer reviewDiscover Applied SciencesCo-author

    Uncertainty-Aware Lung Cancer Histopathology Classification Under Cross-Dataset Distribution Shift

    K. K. Eepuri, B. Kondreddy, N. Vytla, Y. Vasimalla, B. S. Vakkalakula

    Lung cancer histopathology classification that reports its own uncertainty, tested on data from a different source than it was trained on.
